🔍 VITAL SIGNALS & INDICATORS

NDVI / EVI

Normalized Difference Vegetation Index. Sudden persistent drop → vegetation loss / mining. Theil-Sen negative slope (< -0.15/yr) indicates degradation.

BSI (Bare Soil)

Bare Soil Index. Sharp rise (+0.15) concurrent with NDVI drop → excavation, soil removal, mining signature.

NTL volatility

Nighttime lights (VIIRS). Coefficient of variation >1.5 in historically dark area → new industrial activity, 24/7 operations.

NDWI & pit lakes

Water index rise >0.30 after mining signature → abandoned quarry lake, regulatory concern.

Z-score anomaly

|Z| > 2.0 vs. historical monthly mean (5+ years) → statistically extreme change, independent of season.

DEM + 100m rule

SRTM elevation cross-referenced with Supreme Court 100m hill rule. Any excavation above 100m → automatic critical flag.

🧪 SPECTRAL INDICES & INTERPRETATION

NDVI = (NIR-RED)/(NIR+RED)

Strong correlation with green biomass. Healthy forest: 0.6–0.8. Drift alert: value drops below 0.2 persistently. Mining signature often sees NDVI <0.15.

BSI = ((SWIR+RED)-(NIR+BLUE))/((SWIR+RED)+(NIR+BLUE))

High BSI (>0.25) indicates bare soil, fallow, or excavation. New mining sites show BSI increase >0.2 within 3 months.

NDWI = (GREEN-NIR)/(GREEN+NIR)

High values (>0.3) indicate open water. Monitors formation of pit lakes in abandoned mines — water accumulation can signal mine closure or illegal water bodies.

NTL radiance (nanoWatts/cm²/sr)

Monthly composite from VIIRS. Background rural: 0–5. Mine site with night activity: >30. Illegal mines often show sudden NTL appearance.

⏱️ CHANGE-POINT & TREND ALGORITHMS

📈 BFAST (Breaks for Additive Seasonal and Trend)

• Decomposes time series into trend, seasonal, and noise components.
• Iteratively detects structural breaks in both trend and seasonality.
• In Aravalli: break in trend = persistent land-cover change (e.g., forest to mine).
• Seasonality break = change in phenological pattern (e.g., irrigated to barren).
• Implemented on Google Earth Engine (GEE) for each 10m pixel.

📉 Theil-Sen estimator (robust slope)

• Non-parametric median of pairwise slopes — insensitive to outliers.
• Used for NDVI trend over 5+ years.
• Slope < -0.015 per month (≈ -0.18/yr) flags degradation.
• Combined with Mann-Kendall test for significance.

🔄 CUSUM & PyCUSP

• Cumulative sum charts for early change detection.
• PyCUSP (Python CUSP) detects subtle shifts in time series mean.
• Applied to NTL and NDVI for rapid onset of activity (mining start).

🌲 Isolation Forest (unsupervised)

• Ensemble method that isolates anomalies via random partitioning.
• Feature space: NDVI Z-score, BSI Z-score, NTL CV, slope, BFAST break magnitude.
• Anomaly score > 0.6 → high-drift candidate.

⚖️ REGULATORY KNOWLEDGE BASE (RAJASTHAN / INDIA)

Mines & Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957

Mandates that all mining must have a valid lease. Our system cross-references digitized lease boundaries from DMG Rajasthan. Outside lease → illegal.

Supreme Court 100m Hill Rule (2018)

Prohibits mining on hills with elevation >100m above local relief in Aravalli region. DEM analysis flags any excavation above this contour.

Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 & Wetland Rules

Mining within 50m of water bodies, forest land, or protected areas is prohibited. GIS buffers automatically trigger violation.

Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980

Diversion of forest land for non-forestry requires central approval. Any drift inside notified forest → critical alert + forest department notification.
